Privacy policy

Cricket Score · last updated 17 November 2026

Cricket Score is a free app for scoring cricket matches and sharing the live score with friends. This page explains what it stores and why.

Without an account

You can score matches without signing in. Your matches and players are then kept only on your phone. If you share a live score, that match's score is published so anyone with the link can watch it.

With an account

When you sign in (with an email code or with Google) we store:

  • Your email address, and your name from Google if you use Google sign-in.
  • The name and phone number you enter in your profile.
  • Your matches, players and groups, so they appear on all your phones.

From Google we ask only for your name, email address and profile picture. We don't read anything else from your Google account.

Groups

People in a group you join can see your name, your phone number, the group's players and its matches and statistics. Players added to a group by other members (name and phone number) are visible to that group's members.

Phone contacts

If you tap "From contacts", your phone's contact picker opens and only the people you choose (name and phone number) are added as players. The app does not upload or read your whole contact list.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and the website is served by Cloudflare. Sign-in emails are sent through Gmail.

Deleting your data

You can edit your profile in the app under "Your account". To delete your account and everything stored with it, email yuvarajeshsp@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we'll delete it within 30 days.
